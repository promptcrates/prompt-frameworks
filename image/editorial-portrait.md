# Editorial portrait — Subject · Atmosphere · Technical

Compatible with: Midjourney, Flux, and similar image models.  
Canonical site page: https://promptcrates.com/guides/how-to-structure-image-prompts

```
SUBJECT:
Adult editorial portrait, one person, mid-30s, short dark hair, wool coat,
looking just off-camera, relaxed jaw, no smile, no extra figures

ATMOSPHERE:
Overcast daylight through a tall window, cool gray interior,
soft cheek-shadow falloff, quiet magazine mood, no beauty-flat fill

TECHNICAL:
--ar 3:2, 85mm look, shallow depth, natural skin texture,
no beauty blur, no fake logos
```

Revision rule: change Atmosphere only if the face is right but the light is wrong. Do not rewrite Subject at the same time.
