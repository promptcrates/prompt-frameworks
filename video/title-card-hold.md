# Title card hold — Subject · Atmosphere · Technical

Use with Runway, Kling, CapCut / Premiere finishing.  
Related guide: https://promptcrates.com/guides/hold-title-card-for-two-full-seconds

```
SUBJECT:
Static title card, two words only, high-contrast type, no extra graphics

ATMOSPHERE:
Quiet, no whoosh, no lens flare, no particle rain

TECHNICAL:
Hold the card for two full seconds before any cut.
Ban 0.5-second flashes. Mute-and-read-aloud check after export.
```
